CoverScope privacy notice

This is a demonstration environment. Use fictional customer and vehicle details. Do not enter payment, identity-document or other confidential information.

Account information

Username accounts store your chosen name, username and a password hash. Google sign-in is optional. Google and Supabase Auth process the account/profile information you authorize, including your account email and provider identity. This application stores a display name, a generated username and a hash of the verified Google identifier to recognize your account.

The sign-in integration requests basic identity/profile access. It does not request access to Gmail or Drive. Google/Supabase access tokens are handled on the server during sign-in and are not stored in browser local storage by this application.

Your comparison activity

Saved comparisons contain the customer/vehicle details you submit, test quotation results and activity timestamps. Regular accounts can access their own saved comparisons. Administrative accounts and authorized system operators manage the service. Do not treat this testing environment as an anonymous service.

Cookies and service providers

Essential cookies maintain your application session, protect form submissions and complete Google sign-in. Hosting, database and optional insurance/vehicle providers process requests needed for the functions you use. Their own privacy terms also apply.

Retention and requests

This version has no self-service account deletion feature. Account and comparison records remain until the operator manages their retention. Contact the person who provided your access link for account or data questions, and review Google's connected-app controls to manage your Google authorization.
